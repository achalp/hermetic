# LinkedIn post (companion to the blog post)

Three weeks ago, installing Hermetic meant cloning a repo.

Today it's a signed desktop app for macOS, Windows, and Linux — with verified
auto-update — plus a one-file extension that turns Claude Desktop into a client
of your local, private AI data analyst. Fifteen stable releases in fifteen days.

The feature I'm proudest of isn't a feature. It's what happened when the first
real installs hit real machines: no log file, missing runtime assets that only
existed on my dev machine, a version label that had been wrong for six
releases. Every one of those gaps now has an assert that makes it impossible
to ship again. Your first ten users are an audit — build so their findings
become permanent.

Also new since launch: connect an entire data catalog (including STAC — we test
against Overture Maps' planetary dataset) as one source and ask questions
across it; every artifact build-provenance attested; the updater key rotated
with proven backup custody; and a security patch turnaround of under a day
when a rustls advisory landed.

Local-first, open source, and the model still never sees your rows.
